Villa Mondragone · Frascati / Monte Porzio Catone · Rome, Italy

Villa Mondragone

Villa Mondragone is the Congress and Representation Centre of the University of Rome Tor Vergata. The historic villa lies in the Tusculan Hills, just outside Frascati, in the municipality of Monte Porzio Catone.

The complex dates back to the sixteenth century and is closely associated with Pope Gregory XIII. Today it hosts conferences, institutional meetings and cultural events in a historic setting overlooking the Roman countryside.

How to reach Villa Mondragone

Current entrance and route inside the park

Via Mondragone 4, 00078 Monte Porzio Catone (Rome), Italy

A
Via Ercole Consalvi, Frascati

Starting from point A, take Via Ercole Consalvi, following the route towards Monte Porzio Catone.

B
Via Mondragone 4, Monte Porzio Catone

At the end of the road, you reach the gate of Villa Mondragone — University of Rome “Tor Vergata”.

C
Villa Mondragone

Follow the road inside the park of Villa Mondragone until you reach the space in front of the building entrance.

From Fiumicino “Leonardo da Vinci” Airport

Direct train — Leonardo Express

Take the fast train to Roma Termini, departing every 30 minutes, then continue from Termini by metro / bus or take a local train to Frascati. The whole trip is about 1 h 30 min. Tickets for both trains can be purchased together by selecting departure from Roma Termini and arrival in Frascati on Trenitalia.

Metropolitan train (FM1) + Metro

Take the FM1 metropolitan train towards Roma Tiburtina. Departures are approximately every 15 minutes from Monday to Saturday and every 30 minutes on Sundays and holidays. Get off at Tuscolana station and continue by metro / bus.

Taxi

A taxi from Fiumicino Airport to Frascati takes about 40 minutes in normal traffic, with a higher risk of delays during busy periods.

From Ciampino Airport

Bus

Take a COTRAL bus to Anagnina metro station, then follow the directions below for the bus towards Rocca Priora.

Train

From Ciampino Airport, take the bus to Ciampino Train Station and from there a local train to Frascati. The whole trip takes approximately 30 minutes to 1 hour, depending on connections. Tickets for both bus and train can be purchased by selecting departure from Ciampino Aeroporto and arrival in Frascati on Trenitalia.

Taxi

A taxi from Ciampino Airport to Frascati takes about 20 minutes in normal traffic.

From Roma Termini Train Station

Train

Take a local train to Frascati. Tickets can be purchased by selecting departure from Roma Termini and arrival in Frascati on Trenitalia.

Metro

Take Metro A to the terminus at Anagnina, then continue by COTRAL bus as described below.

From Anagnina — Metro A terminus

The metro ticket can be purchased at any metro station. At Anagnina, take the COTRAL bus towards Rocca Priora via Monte Porzio Catone and get off at the Hotel Villa Vecchia stop. Stops are on request, so it is advisable to ask the driver. The COTRAL bus ticket can be purchased at Anagnina station.

By car or taxi

From the A1 Milan–Naples motorway or from the A24 Roma–L'Aquila / Pescara motorway, follow the direction to Roma Sud and take the Monte Porzio Catone exit.

From the motorway exit, turn right and take Via Fontana Candida. At the roundabout follow the signs for Monte Porzio Catone; at the second roundabout turn left into Via Frascati 51, then continue towards the current entrance at Via Mondragone 4.

From Rome city centre

Take the G.R.A. towards the A1 Rome–Naples motorway and exit at Monte Porzio Catone. From the motorway exit, follow the directions above for arrival by car.

From Frascati

We suggest that participants share a taxi or Uber from Frascati to Villa Mondragone. The drive takes approximately 5–10 minutes.

You can reserve a taxi:

  1. with the itTaxi app;
  2. by calling +39 06 87377 or +39 06 91522;
  3. through your hotel reception;
  4. directly with taxi drivers parked in front of the train station or the main square.

Walking: Villa Mondragone is about a 30-minute walk from Frascati. The route is relatively steep uphill and participants should also be aware that wild boars may occasionally be encountered along the road in the park.
