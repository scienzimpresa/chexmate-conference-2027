Villa Mondragone

Villa Mondragone is the Congress and Representation Centre of the University of Rome Tor Vergata. The historic villa lies in the Tusculan Hills, just outside Frascati, in the municipality of Monte Porzio Catone.

The complex dates back to the sixteenth century and is closely associated with Pope Gregory XIII. Today it hosts conferences, institutional meetings and cultural events in a historic setting overlooking the Roman countryside.

Recommended route: Rome → Frascati by train

For most participants, the simplest option is to reach Roma Termini and continue from there by regional train to Frascati. This avoids the more complicated combination of metro and suburban buses.

Train connections and tickets can be checked on Trenitalia. When planning your journey, use Frascati as your final railway destination.

From Fiumicino “Leonardo da Vinci” Airport

We recommend taking the Leonardo Express from Fiumicino Airport to Roma Termini. From Termini, continue by regional train to Frascati.

It is often convenient to search for the complete journey from Fiumicino Aeroporto to Frascati directly on Trenitalia, which will show the connection through Roma Termini.

A direct taxi from Fiumicino to Frascati is also possible, but travel time can vary substantially with traffic.

From Ciampino Airport

From Ciampino Airport, there are two practical options:

  • reach Roma Termini by airport shuttle/bus, then take the regional train to Frascati;
  • reach Ciampino railway station by local shuttle/bus and continue by train towards Frascati, according to the timetable.

Check the complete itinerary on Trenitalia before travelling.

A taxi from Ciampino Airport to Frascati is also a convenient option and is usually much shorter than the journey from Fiumicino.

Arriving in Rome by train

If your train arrives at Roma Termini, continue directly with the regional train to Frascati.

If you arrive at another major station in Rome, such as Roma Tiburtina or Roma Ostiense, the simplest option is generally to reach Roma Termini first and continue from there to Frascati.

From Frascati to Villa Mondragone

From Frascati, we recommend sharing a taxi to Villa Mondragone. The drive takes approximately 5–10 minutes.

You can reserve a taxi:

  1. with the itTaxi app;
  2. by calling +39 06 87377 or +39 06 91522;
  3. through your hotel reception;
  4. directly with taxi drivers parked near the railway station or the main square.

Conference transport: the LOC plans to organise an on-request car service / shared lifts for participants who need help reaching Villa Mondragone from Frascati. Details and availability will be communicated closer to the conference.

Walking: Villa Mondragone is about a 30-minute walk from Frascati. The route is uphill and can be relatively steep; participants should also be aware that wild boars may occasionally be encountered along the road in the park.

By car or taxi

From the A1 Milan–Naples motorway or from the A24 Roma–L'Aquila / Pescara motorway, follow the direction to Roma Sud and take the Monte Porzio Catone exit.

From the motorway exit, turn right and take Via Fontana Candida. At the roundabout follow the signs for Monte Porzio Catone; at the second roundabout turn left into Via Frascati 51, then continue towards the current entrance at Via Mondragone 4.
