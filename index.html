Astrophysics and Cosmology of Galaxy Clusters and Groups:

from to NewAthena

14–18 June 2027 Villa Mondragone, Frascati

About the conference

The first CHEX-MATE conference will bring together the galaxy cluster and group community to discuss recent progress in astrophysics and cosmology, from the legacy of CHEX-MATE to the science opportunities of the next generation of X-ray observatories.

The meeting will cover the thermodynamics and mass assembly of clusters and groups, mass calibration and cosmology, multi-wavelength observations, numerical simulations, and the connection between present facilities and future missions such as NewAthena.

Science themes

01

Thermodynamics of the ICM

Temperature, density, entropy and pressure profiles from cluster cores to the outskirts.

02

Mass calibration & cosmology

Hydrostatic masses, weak lensing and the systematics that shape cluster cosmology.

03

Cluster assembly & mergers

Dynamical state, shocks, turbulence and the imprint of hierarchical structure formation.

04

Multi-wavelength synergies

Joint science with radio, SZ, optical/IR surveys and numerical simulations.
