Pre-registration

Pre-registration and abstract submission deadline: 28 February 2027

This form is a pre-registration for the CHEX-MATE Conference 2027. No payment is required at this stage.

The expected registration fee is approximately €350–400. We are currently seeking additional financial support with the aim of keeping the fee as close as possible to €350. The registration fee will include lunches from Tuesday to Friday.

The final fee and payment instructions will be communicated after the scientific programme has been announced. Participants will then be invited to complete the registration by making the payment.
