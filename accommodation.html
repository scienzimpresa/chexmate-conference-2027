Where to stay

We recommend staying in Frascati. It offers the most convenient base for the conference: Villa Mondragone can be reached on foot or easily by taxi/car, while the town centre has many restaurants, cafés and evening options. Frascati is also directly connected to Roma Termini by local train.

Staying in Rome is also possible, but participants should allow at least 45 minutes for the train commute, plus the transfer from Frascati station to Villa Mondragone.

Hotel Colonna

Hotel Flora
