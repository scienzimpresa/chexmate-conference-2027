About Villa Mondragone

Villa Mondragone is the Congress and Representation Centre of the University of Rome Tor Vergata. The historic villa lies in the Tusculan Hills, just outside Frascati, in the municipality of Monte Porzio Catone.

The complex dates back to the sixteenth century and is closely associated with Pope Gregory XIII. Today it hosts conferences, institutional meetings and cultural events in a historic setting overlooking the Roman countryside.

How to get there

From central Rome

Regional trains connect Roma Termini with Frascati. From Frascati station, the simplest option for the final part of the journey is a short taxi ride to Villa Mondragone.

Via Metro A / Anagnina

From Rome, Metro A reaches its eastern terminus at Anagnina. COTRAL services towards Monte Porzio Catone / Rocca Priora provide connections towards the Villa area. We will publish the most convenient 2027 connections closer to the conference.

From the airports

From Fiumicino, a convenient public-transport route is the Leonardo Express to Roma Termini, followed by a regional train to Frascati. From Ciampino, taxi transfer is the most direct option; public transport via Anagnina is also possible.

By car or taxi

Use the Monte Porzio Catone exit and continue towards Via Frascati 51. For taxi drop-off, ask to be taken to the main entrance of Villa Mondragone rather than the external gate on Via Frascati.

Travel note: local public-transport timetables may change before June 2027. Detailed travel instructions and recommended connections will be updated closer to the meeting.
